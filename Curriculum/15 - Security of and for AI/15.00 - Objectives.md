# Objectives for Module 15 - Security of and for AI

This module tests the understanding a developer has about authentication (various authentication protocols, implementation of the various protocols) and managing credentials (using identity providers, storing credentials and secrets, secure use of service accounts / service principals / managed identities).

A successful candidate can:

- Identify and mitigate OWASP LLM Top 10 (2025) vulnerabilities
- Implement defenses against prompt injection and system prompt leakage
- Secure vector databases and RAG implementations against embedding attacks
- Apply controls for excessive agency, unbounded consumption, and misinformation risks
- Design guardrails and content filtering for AI-generated outputs
- Understand general architecture of modern AI systems
- Understand how to perform basic checks during development and testing of AI systems

---

## Expected Competencies

Candidates can integrate AI into CI/CD pipelines, code reviews, and tests during development, apply AI as a verification framework for assessments, such as penetration testing and audits and reference specific requirements when evaluating AI vendors and third-party models during procurement
