# Objectives for Module 8 - Data Protection and Privacy

This module tests the developer for inclusion of data protection and privacy in application development

A successful canditdate can:

- Implement data classification and handling procedures based on sensitivity levels
- Apply encryption and protection techniques for data at rest and in use
- Implement data minimization and anonymization techniques
- Design and implement secure data deletion and retention policies
- Understand regulatory requirements and translate them into technical controls
- The importance of protecting sensitive and personal data
- is aware of data classification and handling requirements
- is aware of and can implement requirements of common privacy principles and regulations (privacy laws, data minimization, consent, purpose limitation, retention)
- Is aware of types of sensitive data (PII, financial data, health data, credentials)
- Implement secure data handling practices during storage, processing, and transmission
- Implement data encryption concepts (at rest and in transit)
- Implement Access control and least privilege principles from data protection and privacy perspective
- Is aware of risks associated with improper data protection (data leakage, unauthorized access, regulatory penalties, reputational damage)

---

## Expected Competencies
Candidates can implement effective data protection and privacy practices, ensuring that sensitive and personal information is securely collected, processed, stored, and transmitted. The candidate enables organizations to reduce the risk of data breaches, support regulatory compliance, enhance user trust, and contribute to building secure and privacy-aware applications.
